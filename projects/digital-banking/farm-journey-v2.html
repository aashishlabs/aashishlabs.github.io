Farm Journey Revamp — Customer Onboarding V2

Revamping the mobile application used by field-level officers to register and onboard rural lending customers at L&T Finance.

50%Faster product delivery through Agile transformation
2Rural lending products managed
V2Field onboarding application revamp

The Context

At L&T Finance, I managed digital transformation initiatives across rural lending products including Farm and WRF. Field-level officers used a mobile application to register and onboard customers before the journey continued into downstream enterprise systems such as Salesforce for credit and operational processing.

The Challenge

The existing field journey needed improvement across usability, process clarity and operational efficiency. The revamp required coordination across business, field operations, product and technology teams while ensuring that the onboarding layer connected effectively with downstream lending processes.

My Role

I led the Farm Journey Revamp initiative to shape Version 2.0 of the field onboarding application. My role included understanding field-user pain points, gathering and prioritising requirements, coordinating with business and technology stakeholders, and steering the product revamp through delivery.

Key Product Work

  • Worked with field and business teams to identify journey gaps and operational pain points.
  • Gathered, structured and prioritised requirements for the Version 2.0 application.
  • Helped improve the customer registration and field onboarding journey.
  • Coordinated across business, operations and technology stakeholders during delivery.
  • Supported Agile transformation practices that improved product delivery speed.

Impact

  • Agile transformation contributed to 50% faster product delivery.
  • Improved the field onboarding experience for rural lending operations.
  • Strengthened the hand-off between field customer onboarding and downstream lending processes.
  • Supported digital transformation across Farm and WRF rural lending products.

Key Learning

My key learning was that field-led financial products must be designed around the realities of frontline users. Clear workflows, efficient data capture, dependable system hand-offs and strong coordination between business and technology are critical to achieving operational scale.
